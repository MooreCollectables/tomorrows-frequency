Free music for creators

Stream-safe background music for videos, streams and podcasts.

You can use any track from the Tomorrows Frequency YouTube channel in your own content for free, including monetised YouTube videos, Twitch and Kick streams, podcasts, Shorts, Reels and TikToks.

All we ask: credit the track

Put this in your video description, stream panel or show notes:

Music: "Track Name" by Tomorrows Frequency – https://www.youtube.com/@TomorrowsFrequency

You can

  • use tracks as background music in videos, streams, podcasts and short-form clips;
  • use them in monetised content;
  • cut, loop, fade or edit them to fit your content.

Please don't

  • re-upload a track on its own, or as a music-only video, playlist or mix on another channel;
  • claim the music as your own or register it with Content ID or any rights-management service;
  • sell or distribute the tracks to streaming services.

No copyright claims

Tomorrows Frequency does not register its tracks with Content ID, so credited use won't get a claim from us. If you ever get a claim on one of our tracks, email us and we'll help sort it out.

About the music

The tracks are made with AI music tools under a paid plan that grants commercial rights, and are hand-picked and released by Tomorrows Frequency. This licence may be updated; the version on this page applies.
